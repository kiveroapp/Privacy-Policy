12. Use by minors

Kivero is intended for preparation for the Brazilian Bar Examination (OAB), and its use is restricted to law graduates or students who are in the final stages of their law degree. Because eligibility for the exam requires higher education as a prerequisite, the app's audience is naturally composed of individuals over 18 years of age. Kivero does not direct its content to minors and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adolescents. If an account belonging to a minor is identified without the proper consent of a parent or legal guardian, the account and corresponding data will be removed as soon as the situation is identified.
